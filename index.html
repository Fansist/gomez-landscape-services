Frequently asked questions.

A few honest answers about how working with the Gomez crew usually goes.

Do you offer one-time cleanups or only ongoing service?

Gomez Landscape Services handles both ongoing weekly maintenance and standalone jobs like cleanups, mulch installation, or irrigation repair. Call and describe what your yard needs, and the crew can tell you what fits.

What areas of San Diego do you serve?

The crew is based near Clairemont Mesa Blvd and works properties across San Diego neighborhoods. If you're not sure whether your address is in range, a quick call will get you an answer.

How do I get a quote or schedule service?

Call 858-229-7252 and describe your property and what you're looking for, regular mowing, irrigation work, a cleanup, or something bigger. There's no online booking form; it's a direct conversation with the crew.

Can you fix an existing sprinkler or irrigation system, or only install new ones?

Both. Sprinkler and irrigation work covers repair of existing systems as well as new design and installation, so it applies whether you have a broken zone or want irrigation added from scratch.

Is Gomez Landscape Services a licensed company or a solo operator?

It's a family-run crew, not a solo operator or a large franchise, the same people who answer the phone are the ones doing the work in your yard. For specific licensing or insurance documentation, ask the crew directly when you call.
